# ASCEND Daily Driver Guide

## Before the real Day 1
1. Complete Operator Synchronization honestly.
2. Verify calorie, protein, water, steps and sleep targets in Settings.
3. Open Fuel and calibrate the approximate katori, serving-spoon and glass sizes used by your mess.
4. Export a backup from Settings.
5. Initialize the campaign only when you are ready for Day 1.

## In the gym
- Open Train.
- Expand each exercise.
- Log load and reps for each completed set.
- Follow the displayed Next Target when form and recovery are normal.
- Do not force a target through pain or broken technique.
- Press Complete Training Day after the session.

## In the mess
- Open Fuel.
- Select only the foods actually available.
- Choose hunger level and meal context.
- Generate the plate.
- Take the suggested household quantities.
- Log the estimated meal.

## Backups
ASCEND stores data in your browser. Export a backup weekly and before:
- clearing browser data,
- reinstalling the app,
- changing phones or laptops,
- testing a new development build.
